# Pass List: Software Engineer I - 103QE, Quartus Engineering

## Knockouts (auto-reject if failed, checked by either HR or ATS)

| # | Knockout | What the JD says |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | US Person (US Citizen or US Permanent Resident) | "Must be a US Person (US Citizen or US Permanent Resident)" |
| K2 | Location / hybrid feasibility in San Diego, CA | "Position is based in San Diego with a hybrid schedule" |
| K3 | BS in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or related discipline (or clearly equivalent) | "BS in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or related discipline" |

## HR / recruiter pass list (what gets a resume past the first screen)

| # | Check | Pass bar |
|---|---|---|
| HR-1 | Degree in CS/CE or related field visible on resume | A degree line that reads as CS, CE, or clearly adjacent |
| HR-2 | At least one of .NET, C#, or C/C++ appears with any supporting context | Keyword present; recruiter-level screen usually does not verify depth |
| HR-3 | Years of experience roughly fits "0-2 years" band or is not wildly disqualifying either direction | Recruiter may flag significant overqualification as a risk (comp mismatch, flight risk) as well as underqualification |
| HR-4 | US work authorization stated or inferable | Resume or application answers show no sponsorship needed |
| HR-5 | Location/hybrid willingness | Resume location or stated willingness to work hybrid in San Diego |

## Hiring manager pass list (what gets a candidate to interview)

| # | Check | Pass bar | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| HM-1 | Real project evidence in .NET/C#/C++ | A bullet describing actual software built in one of these languages, not just a skills-list keyword | M1 |
| HM-2 | UI framework experience (WPF/WinUI/Avalonia/Blazor) | A bullet describing a UI built with one of these | M2 |
| HM-3 | Design patterns applied (OOP/MVVM/Producer-Consumer) | Evidence the candidate applied a named pattern in a real system, not just terminology | M3 |
| HM-4 | Asynchronous programming in a real system | A bullet showing async/await, tasks, or futures used in production code | M4 |
| HM-5 | Software integrating with hardware or control systems | A bullet describing software that controlled, monitored, or interfaced with physical hardware | M5 |
| HM-6 | Testing/debugging discipline | Named test frameworks, or a described unit/integration test process | M6 |
| HM-7 | Cross-disciplinary team collaboration | Evidence of working alongside hardware/mechanical/electrical engineers, not purely software teams | H1 |
| HM-8 | Communication/presentation ability | Mainly judged in interview; resume evidence is a weak secondary signal only | M7 |

## Ranking only (does not gate pass/fail, used to prioritize among passing candidates)

- N1: Communication protocols (UART, EtherCAT, UDP, TCP/IP, MODBUS)
- N2: Software architecture/design ownership
- N3: Hardware-in-the-loop testing experience
- H2: Documentation/technical writing evidence
- H3: Evidence of ramping into unfamiliar technical domains quickly
