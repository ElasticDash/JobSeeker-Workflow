## Description

- As a Backend Engineer at Cardless, you’ll work across the systems that run our partners’ card programs: applications and underwriting, accounts and ledgers, transactions and payments, rewards, risk, and the APIs our partners build against
- We keep this role deliberately general. There is more work than there are people, and the engineers who do best here are the ones who move to wherever the problem is. There are three kinds of work on our roadmap, and most people touch all three:
- Building things that don’t exist yet, usually because a partner needs them to launch
- Scaling and hardening what we already run, so the platform holds up as we add partners and volume
- Laying groundwork for products we haven’t launched, including bringing more of the core financial infrastructure in-house
- Design and build backend systems that move real money, make credit decisions, and serve hundreds of thousands of cardholders
- Take projects from ambiguity to production: scoping, design, implementation, testing, rollout, and the operational life that follows
- Work across domains rather than inside a single service, going where the highest-value problem is
- Partner with product, risk, compliance, operations, and our partner and vendor counterparts to ship things that hold up in a regulated environment
- Improve reliability, observability, and developer experience as you go, and leave each system better than you found it
