6. Overlap between Codametrix (started 2025-09) and the Master's program (ending 2025-12) is plausible (student working near graduation) but should read consistently on any resume version.

## Fit direction

Best fit: mid-level production LLM/RAG/AI engineering roles in healthcare or regulated-data contexts, where prompt engineering, retrieval, and production deployment are core to the role; strongest when the title target is "AI Engineer" / "ML Engineer" rather than "Senior."
